# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our commitment / 我们的承诺

We want Fuyue Convert to be a welcoming, practical, and technically honest
community. Participation must remain respectful regardless of experience,
identity, background, language, location, or preferred toolchain.

我们希望 Fuyue Convert 成为友善、务实、尊重技术事实的社区。无论经验、身份、背景、
语言、地区或工具偏好如何，所有参与者都应受到尊重。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal attributes.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while protecting users.
- State uncertainty, format limitations, sample provenance, and test gaps.
- Respect privacy, copyright, trademarks, licenses, and coordinated disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope, compatibility, quality level, and
  release safety even when proposing an alternative.

- 讨论方案和证据，不攻击个人。
- 提供可执行的反馈，在保护用户的前提下善意协作。
- 如实说明不确定性、格式限制、样本来源和测试缺口。
- 尊重隐私、版权、商标、许可证和协调披露规则。
- 尊重维护者对范围、兼容性、质量等级与发布安全作出的最终决定。

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private
data, publishing unlicensed samples, security exploitation, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable.

骚扰、威胁、歧视性表达、故意暴露隐私、发布无授权样本、利用安全问题以及持续扰乱
协作均不可接受。

## Scope and enforcement / 适用范围与执行

This policy applies to Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, releases, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close contributions, restrict participation, or report serious abuse
to the hosting platform. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact,
repetition, and the safety of affected people.

本准则适用于 Issue、Pull Request、Discussion、发布活动及代表项目参与的其他空间。
维护者可编辑或删除内容、关闭贡献、限制参与，并将严重滥用报告给托管平台。执行时会
综合考虑上下文、影响、重复程度和受影响人员的安全。

Report conduct concerns privately to `520fuyue@gmail.com` with the subject
`[Fuyue Convert Conduct]`. Do not put personal evidence in a public Issue.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical, subject to safety and legal
requirements.

行为问题请私下发送至 `520fuyue@gmail.com`，主题使用
`[Fuyue Convert Conduct]`。请勿在公开 Issue 中放置个人证据；在安全和法律允许范围内，
报告会尽量保密处理。

This policy is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
