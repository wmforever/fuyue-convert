Fuyue Convert - Eclipse Temurin corresponding source offer
==========================================================

The Lite desktop package includes a jlink runtime derived from Eclipse Temurin
17.0.20.1+1. The exact corresponding OpenJDK source archive is available at:

https://github.com/adoptium/temurin17-binaries/releases/download/jdk-17.0.20.1%2B1/OpenJDK17U-jdk-sources_17.0.20.1_1.tar.gz

SHA-256:
21e2a065d244ab048e737f21af5d1fc74daaeb6707de36477ead8db1dca71214

For at least three years after distribution of this binary, a copy of the same
corresponding source may also be requested through the public project issue
tracker at https://github.com/wmforever/fuyue-convert/issues . No charge beyond
the reasonable cost of physically providing the source will be required.
