Fuyue Convert does not use electron-builder's NSIS target or template. Its
tracked core-only script uses the NSIS 3.12 built-in File, CreateShortCut, and
WriteUninstaller instructions with zlib compression and user-level execution.

bzip2 module, or the LZMA module.
